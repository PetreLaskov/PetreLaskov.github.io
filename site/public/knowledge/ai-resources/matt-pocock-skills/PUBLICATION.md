# Public study edition

Published 29 September 2026 as part of Petre Laskov's The Knowledge Project → AI Resources.

This study was commissioned and selected for publication by Petre Laskov. OpenAI Codex produced the analysis, candidate refactors, original proposals and development evaluation with multiple AI agents. The AI-generated judgments are not presented as independent human review or Matt Pocock's endorsement.

The original skill collection is Matt Pocock's, preserved at commit c55ee46073ed923f86ce59a5eb3b6d895095d1b7 under its MIT license. Attribution and license copies remain with the source and derived packages. Newly proposed instructions are identified separately from Matt's originals.

All 38 chapters, 38 refactors, 38 current original proposals, exact package diffs, reader data and combined Markdown remain unchanged from the completed local edition. The website adds navigation, attribution and publication metadata around the reader. The unchanged offline reader is also available as COMPENDIUM.html.

Only host-specific filesystem paths in tooling, diagnostics and reproduction notes have been normalized for publication. One private workspace continuation note, unrelated to the study content, is omitted. publication-export.json records hashes and every normalized file. Pinned source bytes and tested skill packages are unchanged.

The suite retained 342 short text responses. Refactors tied the source in 36 skill groups and scored lower in two; the no-skill control was also strong. This development evaluation does not demonstrate a general improvement in collaboration. Three packages received versioned repairs with separate targeted evidence. Read the method and limitations alongside any grade.
