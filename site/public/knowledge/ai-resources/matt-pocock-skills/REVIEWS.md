# Independent reviews and revisions

Two agents inspected the complete collection independently of the chapter advocacy. One compared all 38 source/refactor packages and support files; the other read all 38 original proposals. These were design reviews, not live workflow tests.

The initial semantic audit found **31 clear, 5 deliberate tradeoffs, and 2 repairs**. Both repairs were implemented with the initial candidates preserved: triage regains authorized closure; wizard fixes required-input and completion behavior. The [wizard regression results](evals/WIZARD-REPAIR.md) distinguish the repaired helper from the text trial of its first version.

The response trial found a third concrete repair: the setup seed could override an existing tracker layout. Its final text gives observed conventions priority and receives a targeted follow-up. Writing-beats also lost partial credit for unsupplied story assumptions; its unchanged instructions receive an isolated retest, while the original score remains visible.

The original-portfolio audit recommended **26 pilots, 8 merges, 2 repairs, and 2 deferrals**. This is a useful challenge to the idea that 38 proposals should mean 38 installations. Two overlapping proposals were replaced with preference-probes and anchored-variation. Instruction-ablation and thesis-stress-test received consequential validity repairs. The remaining merge/defer judgments remain visible in each chapter rather than being edited into universal praise.

## Portfolio judgment

Verdicts: 'pilot' means the design has a sufficiently concrete mechanism to justify a bounded behavior test, not that it is proven valuable or should immediately be installed. 'merge' means preserve useful mechanisms inside a clearer family. 'repair' means fix a consequential validity defect before interpreting a pilot. 'defer' means the text is sound guidance but a separate skill has not yet earned its routing and context cost.

The portfolio is strongest where it forces an inspectable contrast: test-the-oracle compares correct and deliberately wrong behavior; resume-probe checks a fresh receiver's artifact-grounded next action; reviewer-rehearsal reconstructs a PR's meaning; rehearse-the-next-change exposes actual obligations under competing designs. These mechanisms can reveal something a fluent explanation alone cannot. They deserve early pilots, along with integration-contracts and decision-preserving-compression.

Thirty-eight source mappings do not justify thirty-eight separately installed skills. The clearest duplicate pairs are counterexample-search/construct-a-counterexample and counterimage/thesis-stress-test. Fixture-truth fits as a diagnostic mode in test-the-oracle. Boundary-cases fits as a classification mode in intent-examples. Consolidation should preserve the stronger local details rather than merely deleting one file.

An uncertainty-to-action family would make routing clearer: assumption-budget selects consequential unknowns; evidence-fork chooses a discriminating observation; reversible-bets designs a sustained reversible trial. Find-the-binding-constraint and find-the-unasked-question are useful entry modes for stalls and framing defects. The first three represent different stages; the latter two do not need competing generic diagnostic identities.

The learning family contains legitimate separable outputs: design-the-learning-sequence orchestrates adaptive practice, misconception-lab authors a diagnostic exercise, and transfer-test assesses application in a new case. Keep them callable at those different scopes, but share assessment principles. Likewise, resume-check validates changing external state while resume-probe tests successor comprehension; neither is a substitute for the other.

Most triggers include sensible exclusions and most procedures stop after a decision-relevant result. The broadest entries still overlap ordinary assistant duties: stop-rule and finish-the-last-mile chiefly encode completion hygiene; find-the-unasked-question can become routine reframing. Trigger testing should include nearby tasks where extra procedure would waste time, not only ideal prompts that explicitly ask for each named mechanism.

Observable artifacts are common, but outcome claims need stricter separation. A runnable example does not establish transfer; a comprehension question does not establish comprehension; a proposed next action does not establish successful continuation; a working interactive model does not establish better judgment. Reader-state-simulation and several fresh-agent probes already acknowledge this boundary. Pilots should preserve it in their reports.

The designs favor clarification, verification, counterexamples, and controlled repair. That is useful but narrower than a major expansion of collaborative capability: many are disciplined microprocedures for known good practice. The portfolio's ambition should be tested by what new usable work or better decisions it enables, not by the number of named thinking moves or the elegance of instructions.

Run a small first wave rather than all 38: choose mechanisms with clear artifacts and nearest-negative cases, preserve task context, compare against ordinary assistance, and count unnecessary questions, duplicate work, and time as costs. Instruction-ablation could support this only after its treatment-contamination defect is repaired. Do not interpret a successful static audit or a few rehearsals as general behavioral effectiveness.
