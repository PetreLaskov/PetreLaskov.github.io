# Rejected-concept memory

One .out-of-scope/<concept>.md per durably rejected enhancement, including enhancement PRs. Record concept, active/superseded status, substantive reason, decision source/date when known, and prior requests.

Do not record already-implemented behavior or temporary lack of time as rejection. Bugs use their issue history rather than this concept store.

Match new requests by meaning and surface the previous reason. The maintainer can confirm, reconsider, or distinguish the case. A match alone does not authorize closure.

On confirmed rejection, append the request to the existing concept or create a concise record, then reference it in the authorized closing explanation. On reconsideration, mark the prior decision superseded and explain what changed; preserve useful rationale without presenting it as current policy. Do not reopen historical issues automatically.

Keep prose readable and sufficient for a future collaborator to understand the decision. This is not a taxonomy project.
