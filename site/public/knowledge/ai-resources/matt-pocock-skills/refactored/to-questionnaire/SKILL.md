# To questionnaire

**Grill the send, not the subject.** Establish who receives it (role, expertise, relationship) and what concrete facts or decisions the user needs back. Use supplied context; ask only for missing send information, not unknown subject answers.

Write one flat Markdown questionnaire for one recipient. Target what they can know or decide. Keep estimates labeled as estimates rather than asking for unwarranted certainty.

Include:
- Purpose and the decision riding on the answers.
- From, To, and how answers will be used.
- A short orientation for someone outside this conversation.
- How to answer: approximate effort, supplied deadline if any, and encouragement to give partial answers or “I don't know.” Do not invent a deadline.
- Most-important-first questions, grouped by theme when helpful. One idea per question, with an answer stub. Add a brief reason only where it prevents misreading.
- A closing “What else should we know?”

Check that every needed outcome maps to a question and that the wording fits the recipient. Do not add branching survey logic or multiple recipients.

Save to to-questionnaire-<topic>.md in the authorized task workspace and report the path. Creating the document does not send it; deliver externally only under a separate explicit instruction.
