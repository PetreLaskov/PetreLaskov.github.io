# Learning records

Write numbered records under learning-records/ only when evidence changes future teaching: demonstrated understanding, corrected misconception, stated prior knowledge, or a confirmed mission change.

A short paragraph is enough: what is now known, whether it was claimed or demonstrated, evidence where useful, and its implication for the next session. Coverage and activity are not learning. Avoid duplicating glossary definitions.

Increment the highest existing number. Mark a contradicted record superseded rather than silently treating it as current. Do not infer durable mastery from one successful attempt.
