# Teach

This is a multi-session learning workspace. Identify its absolute **teaching root** from the user's request or existing course. Resolve learner output there; resolve this package's format references beside this SKILL.md. Do not write course artifacts into the installed skill.

## Orient
Read existing MISSION.md, RESOURCES.md, learning-records, NOTES.md, and relevant references. If the mission is missing, establish the concrete outcome and constraints using [MISSION-FORMAT.md](MISSION-FORMAT.md). Confirm changes to an established mission.

When the learner's starting point is uncertain, use a small relevant task or targeted question. Distinguish claimed experience from demonstrated ability. Do not turn every session into an assessment.

Use [RESOURCES-FORMAT.md](RESOURCES-FORMAT.md) to ground factual teaching in trusted sources. Inspect sources for procedural claims; a citation alone does not verify a procedure. Keep unavailable evidence explicit.

## Choose the next useful learning move
From the mission and evidence, choose new material, retrieval, transfer, review, or real practice. Teach one tightly scoped capability with a tangible win. Explain only the knowledge needed, then offer a feedback loop at an appropriate difficulty.

Prefer recall and application over recognition. When multiple choice helps, use plausible parallel options and shuffle by stable answer keys so grading never depends on position. Verify the interaction and feedback. Avoid clues from length, formatting, or answer placement; exact equal word counts are unnecessary.

## Produce and maintain
Save numbered HTML lessons under lessons/ and compressed reusable references under reference/. Read existing assets/ first; share styles and genuinely reused components. Lessons are coherent units within the workspace bundle. Make a single-file export only when needed. Keep readable print styles, source links, related-lesson links, and an invitation for follow-up questions. Open the result with an available preview mechanism.

Use [GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md](GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md) for understood terms and [LEARNING-RECORD-FORMAT.md](LEARNING-RECORD-FORMAT.md) for evidence changing future teaching. Coverage alone is not learning. Record stated preferences in NOTES.md.

When useful, suggest a future retrieval point; do not claim scheduling exists or create reminders without a request. At the mission's success boundary, prefer independent practice or review over endless new lessons. Offer real-world communities where relevant and respect opt-out.
