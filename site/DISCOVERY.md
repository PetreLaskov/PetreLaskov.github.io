# Search and AI-search discovery

The Matt Pocock study keeps its full interactive reader and adds 38 permanent chapter pages, a chapter index and an evaluation-method/results page. These derive from the existing study, rather than new keyword-targeted variants. Important content and links are available without JavaScript.

Each study page has a descriptive title, description, canonical URL, visible AI/source attribution, publication date, breadcrumb markup and ordinary CreativeWork/Article metadata. No synthetic ratings, expert credentials or human-review claims are added. The sitemap includes all canonical study pages with their real publication date. Existing public archive routes remain in the merged sitemap.

The existing robots policy allows crawling, including Googlebot, Bingbot and OAI-SearchBot. Training-crawler preferences were not changed. Eligibility, indexing, ranking and actual AI citations are different outcomes; no crawler or metadata setting guarantees them.

IndexNow configuration is in content/indexnow.json with a verification file scoped to /knowledge/ai-resources/. After a successful deployment, submit only added/changed public URLs in that scope. A 200 means received; a 202 means received with key validation pending. Neither means indexed. The verification file must be publicly readable for the protocol and is not an account credential.

Google Search Console ownership, the site's generative-AI inclusion setting, and search-performance reporting have not been verified in this task. If a verified property is available later, inspect indexing and submitted sitemap status there, and measure actual relevant impressions/citations before attributing a benefit to these changes. No new analytics tracking or recurring monitor was added.
