# The Main Practice Routes

The mistake is to read this as a collection: more methods, more advanced methods, a fuller toolkit. A practitioner lost in pain, racing thought, sleepiness, grief, pleasure, or plain choice-paralysis does not need one more technique. They need the one handle that fits what is actually happening. So the art here is not collecting routes; it is matching the route to the live situation.

The main routes in this atlas are:

- [The Five Ways](five-ways.md): the older Basic Mindfulness route map.
- [Noting](noting.md): acknowledge and focus on sensory events.
- [Do Nothing](do-nothing.md): drop noticed intentions to control attention.
- [Focus on Rest](focus-on-rest.md): notice restful aspects of experience.
- [Nurture Positive](nurture-positive.md): create, hold, and spread wholesome patterns.
- [Way of Thoughts and Emotions](way-of-thoughts-and-emotions.md): work with mental image, talk, and emotional Feel.
- [Way of Physical Senses](way-of-physical-senses.md): work with sight, sound, and body sensation.
- [Way of Tranquility](way-of-tranquility.md): refresh through Rest and release through Do Nothing.
- [Way of Flow](way-of-flow.md): notice change, movement, and force.
- [Way of Human Goodness](way-of-human-goodness.md): reconstruct positive human qualities.

Before choosing a route, check the scale of the problem.

| Scale | Useful question | First move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | "How do these routes belong to one system?" | read [The Routes](the-routes.md) or [The Five Ways](five-ways.md) |
| Session container | "Should I stay with one method, run a sequence, or branch?" | use [Choosing a Practice Route](choosing-a-practice-route.md) |
| Live sensory object | "What is asking for attention now?" | use the route matrix below |
| Method tuning | "Is the route right but the controls are off?" | adjust effort, coverage, zoom, or turn-toward/turn-away before changing the whole path |
| Safety or support | "Should meditation optimization wait?" | read [Practice Method Safety](practice-method-safety.md) or [Safety, Scope, and Accountability](safety-scope-and-accountability.md) |

## Route Matrix

| Live situation | Likely route | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vague, fused, or confusing experience | [Noting](noting.md) | labels and focus ranges increase clarity |
| Racy, over-managed practice | [Do Nothing](do-nothing.md) | exposes and releases the control impulse |
| Agitation or intensity needs support | [Focus on Rest](focus-on-rest.md) | finds restful factors without denying the challenge |
| Dryness, nihilism, or weak positive affect | [Nurture Positive](nurture-positive.md) | trains constructive Feel/Image/Talk |
| Emotion, self-talk, memory, fantasy | [Way of Thoughts and Emotions](way-of-thoughts-and-emotions.md) | decomposes inner sensory strands |
| Pain, posture, sound, visual field, activity | [Way of Physical Senses](way-of-physical-senses.md) | grounds practice in outer sense contact |
| Need for rest, non-effort, or release of control | [Way of Tranquility](way-of-tranquility.md) | distinguishes Relative Rest from Absolute Rest |
| Change, vibration, pressure, vanishing | [Way of Flow](way-of-flow.md) | turns impermanence into an object |
| Service, love, conduct, repair | [Way of Human Goodness](way-of-human-goodness.md) | tests practice by the person it forms |
| Too many methods or constant switching | [Choosing a Practice Route](choosing-a-practice-route.md) | turns method choice into one technique, a planned sequence, or a clear branch rather than a status game |
| Fear, craving, pride, or confusion about practice effects | [Recycle the Reaction](recycle-the-reaction.md) | makes the reaction to calm, Flow, Gone, no-self, or intensity the next object when workable |
| Hard choice between facing and stabilizing | [Turn Toward and Turn Away](turn-toward-and-turn-away.md) | keeps direct contact and support branches distinct |
| Racy, spacey, strained, or vague practice | [Effort Regulation](effort-regulation.md) | adjusts bearing down and easing up |

The behavior check sits outside the matrix: if insight is not improving conduct, the next move is not a subtler technique but ordinary behavior work and accountability.

One useful rule cuts through the whole menu: pick the simplest route that makes the next five minutes clearer and safer. The "best" route is not the most advanced; it is the one that increases CCE without losing ordinary responsiveness.

Mixed practice is not automatically shallow. It becomes coherent when the practitioner knows whether they are using one route deeply, moving through a deliberate workout, or branching because the live situation changed.

For the next session, reduce the whole menu to one container:

| Container | Use when | Simple form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One route | the menu is overwhelming, or one issue clearly dominates | choose one method and check whether CCE improves |
| Planned sequence | several capacities need deliberate exercise | move through a short sequence, such as clarity, rest, then positive reconstruction |
| Responsive branch | the live situation changes in an obvious way | name the reason for switching before switching |
| Pause or support | functioning, safety, conduct, or ordinary care is the real issue | stop optimizing technique and handle the practical need first |

## When Route Choice Goes Wrong

Do not treat method choice as identity: one route can go deep, and broad practice is also useful. Switching is not failure - but switching to avoid a difficulty is bypass, and collecting routes for their own sake is the same avoidance dressed as diligence. No method overrides ordinary care.
