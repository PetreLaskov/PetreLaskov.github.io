# The Shinzen Practice Atlas

There is one move under everything here: meet what is actually happening with concentration, sensory clarity, and equanimity until the experience can complete. Shinzen's system is a way of making that move practical: first with ordinary sensations, then with thought and emotion, then with Flow, Gone, self, Source language, behavior, and service. This atlas is for serious practitioners and careful readers who already know that meditation can change a life, and who want the architecture without the inflation. It promises a walkable map. It refuses to turn private states into proof, therapy, teacher authority, or metaphysical certainty.

## The Path

Read these in order when you want the living spine rather than lookup.

1. [The One Move](the-one-move.md) - complete experience as the whole gesture: contact, clarity, equanimity, time, and the loss of binding.
2. [The Three Skills](the-three-skills.md) - concentration, sensory clarity, and equanimity as three independent axes with distinct tastes.
3. [The Sensory Interface](the-sensory-interface.md) - See / Hear / Feel, Feel / Image / Talk, the Sensory Grid, Rest, Flow, and Gone as usable coordinates.
4. [The Routes](the-routes.md) - the Five Ways and the main ways to start without turning route choice into overchoice.
5. [Impermanence](impermanence-path.md) - Flow, Gone, Space, Expansion-Contraction, dissolution, and the edge where experience stops feeling solid.
6. [No-Self Without Erasing the Person](no-self-without-erasing-the-person.md) - de-coagulation, not self-hatred; the self as activity and the person returning warmer.
7. [Source, Zero, and the Honest Edge](source-zero-and-the-honest-edge.md) - intimacy with Source language without pretending the afterglow is proof.
8. [The Return](the-return.md) - operational enlightenment as the marketplace test: conduct, repair, service, and ordinary functioning.
9. [The Aim](the-aim.md) - Total Happiness, condition-independent happiness, love, human goodness, and service as the long arc.
10. [Going Deep Safely](going-deep-safely.md) - intensity, bypass, DPDR, the pit of the void, teacher pressure, and when to stop optimizing meditation.
