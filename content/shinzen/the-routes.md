# The Routes

The menus exist to get you practicing, not to keep you shopping. One route, taken deeply enough with CCE, can train the whole path; Shinzen offers many doors only because different moments need different handles.

The **Five Ways** are five broad ways to apply the one move:

- **[Way of Thoughts and Emotions](way-of-thoughts-and-emotions.md)**: untangle and be free. Work with Image, Talk, and emotional Feel.
- **[Way of Physical Senses](way-of-physical-senses.md)**: anchor and merge. Work with sight, sound, touch, posture, movement, pain, and the physical world.
- **[Way of Tranquility](way-of-tranquility.md)**: refresh and release. Work with Rest and pleasant calm.
- **[Way of Flow](way-of-flow.md)**: dissolve and digest. Work with movement, vibration, change, and energy.
- **[Way of Human Goodness](way-of-human-goodness.md)**: love and serve. Construct positive Feel and let practice return as care.

They are not stages, and not personality types. They are balances. Practice only one and the rest can starve: stay only with untangling and love goes hungry; only with rest, and hard material gets quietly avoided; only with flow, and it slides into chasing energy; only with goodness, and grief gets bypassed. The route is chosen by live fit, not by rank.

Under the Ways sit the techniques, each a different gear for training CCE. **[Noting](noting.md)** bears down: acknowledge a sensory event and know it through focused contact. **[Do Nothing](do-nothing.md)** eases up: let whatever happens happen, and the moment you notice a voluntary intention to control attention, drop that intention. **[Focus on Rest](focus-on-rest.md)** works the restful flavors until calm becomes a usable object. **[Nurture Positive](nurture-positive.md)** strikes the emotional body with a positive thought like a bell, then follows the resonance. **[Auto Output](auto-output-practice.md)** watches movement, speech, chant, or thought organize itself without a felt controller.

A good route reduces overchoice. It gives the session a container, lets feedback accumulate, and makes tuning possible. The live choices in a sit are rarely infinite: turn toward or turn away, bear down or ease up, narrow or widen, stay or switch, keep going or stop and get help.

The test is never whether a route sounds advanced. It is whether the route trains CCE and improves function. If a plain route does that, keep digging in it. If a beautiful route increases confusion, compulsion, dissociation, or avoidance, change it.

## Before You Commit To A Route

Run a candidate route past these five before you build a month of practice around it. If you cannot answer one, that gap is the thing to fix first.

| Question | What a workable answer sounds like |
| --- | --- |
| What am I training? | one or more CCE skills you can name |
| What is the object? | a focus range clear enough to keep returning to |
| How long is the experiment? | a bounded container, not "until it works" |
| What would make me tune it? | feedback specific enough to act on |
| What would make me stop? | safety and function signals named in advance |
