# Glossary

This glossary gives quick public meanings. Each term needs the linked page for context.

## Core Terms

**CCE**: concentration, sensory clarity, and equanimity working together. See [Mindfulness as CCE](mindfulness-as-cce.md).

**Mindfulness**: in Shinzen's precise practice use, mindful awareness means CCE working together. The word can also name practices, a path, a public movement, or a translation choice, so context matters. See [Mindfulness as CCE](mindfulness-as-cce.md) and [The Routes](the-routes.md).

**Claim tiers**: the atlas's public sorting rule for whether a sentence is Shinzen's teaching, compiled synthesis, editorial inference, speculative extension, or not established here. The same page also names the atlas's source families and quotation posture. Use it before turning practice language into proof, authority, clinical advice, science, or metaphysics. See [Source and Claim Tiers](source-and-claim-tiers.md).

**Basic Mindfulness**: Shinzen's older manual architecture, including CCE, the Five Ways, the Sensory Grid, core techniques, practice cycles, and life aims. It is an option field, not a demand to master every route before practicing. See [The Routes](the-routes.md).

**Five Ways**: five thematic practice routes: thoughts and emotions, physical senses, tranquility, Flow, and human goodness. See [The Five Ways](five-ways.md).

**Sensory Grid**: the expanded See/Hear/Feel grammar that crosses sensory channels with In, Out, Rest, Flow, and Gone. See [The Sensory Grid](sensory-grid.md).

**Concentration power**: the ability to focus on what matters when needed. See [Concentration Power](concentration-power.md).

**Mysticism as concentration**: Shinzen's cross-tradition frame in which capital-M mysticism means concentration-heavy contemplative practice that may open unitive, no-self, or Source language. It is not proof that traditions are identical or that unusual states carry authority. See [The Teacher and the Lineage](teacher-and-lineage.md) and [Concentration Power](concentration-power.md).

**Sensory clarity**: the ability to distinguish and track components of experience. See [Sensory Clarity](sensory-clarity.md).

**Discrimination and unification**: the principle that sensory distinctions and unity are not rivals. Clear components can digest into Flow, spaciousness, or a less split self/world field, while healthy unity remains free to use distinctions again. See [Sensory Clarity](sensory-clarity.md) and [Deconstruction and Reconstruction](deconstruction-and-reconstruction.md).

**Equanimity**: noninterference with sensory events, without suppression, numbness, passivity, or forced endurance. See [Equanimity](equanimity.md).

**Equanimity versus suppression**: the safety distinction between sensory noninterference and shutdown, numbness, passivity, compliance, dissociation, or unsafe endurance. See [Equanimity Versus Suppression](equanimity-versus-suppression.md).

**Equanimity training ladder**: practical supports for noninterference, including relaxed body, softer talk, gentle labels, spontaneous drops, second-order equanimity, and background permission. See [Equanimity Training Ladder](equanimity-training-ladder.md).

**See/Hear/Feel**: Shinzen's simplified practice vocabulary for visual, auditory, and body/emotional experience. See [See/Hear/Feel and the Sensory Grid](see-hear-feel-and-the-sensory-grid.md).

**Feel, Image, Talk**: shorthand for emotional body sensation, mental image, and mental talk. See [Feel, Image, Talk](feel-image-talk.md).

**Way of Thoughts and Emotions**: the practice route for working with inner Image, Talk, and emotional Feel when anxiety, anger, grief, shame, self-talk, memory, fantasy, or answer-hunger is active. See [Way of Thoughts and Emotions](way-of-thoughts-and-emotions.md).

**Way of Physical Senses**: Shinzen's outer practice route through physical sight, physical sound, and physical body sensation, including grounding, task contact, movement, listening, and carefully bounded pain or discomfort practice. See [Way of Physical Senses](way-of-physical-senses.md).

**Inner sensory system**: Shinzen's model of inner experience as mental image, mental talk, and emotional body sensation, used for emotion, selfing, no-self, and positive reconstruction. See [The Inner Sensory System](inner-sensory-system.md).

**Noting**: acknowledging a sensory event and focusing on it. See [Noting](noting.md).

**Do Nothing**: dropping noticed voluntary intentions to control attention. See [Do Nothing](do-nothing.md).

**Focus on Rest**: noticing restful aspects of visual, auditory, and body experience. See [Focus on Rest](focus-on-rest.md).

**Way of Tranquility**: the Basic Mindfulness route through Relative Rest and Absolute Rest. See [Way of Tranquility](way-of-tranquility.md).

**Nurture Positive**: deliberately creating, holding, and spreading wholesome subjective patterns. See [Nurture Positive](nurture-positive.md).

**Way of Human Goodness**: Shinzen's constructive route for rebuilding positive Feel, Image, Talk, behavior, cognition, ideals, and service so no-self practice does not become anti-human. See [Way of Human Goodness](way-of-human-goodness.md) and [Nurture Positive](nurture-positive.md).

**Turn toward and turn away**: the choice to contact a challenge directly, stabilize elsewhere while permitting it in the background, focus on change when change is available, or stop optimizing practice when safety and support should lead. See [Turn Toward and Turn Away](turn-toward-and-turn-away.md).

**Effort regulation**: adjusting between bearing down and easing up, such as Noting when practice is spacey or Do Nothing when practice is racy. See [Effort Regulation](effort-regulation.md).

**Focus coverage**: how attention covers a selected range: free-floating, systematic inventory, even coverage, or broad field-clearing. See [Focus Coverage Strategies](focus-coverage-strategies.md).

**Zooming**: a Noting option that adjusts spatial scope by zooming in, zooming out, or holding local and global awareness together. See [Zooming](zooming.md).

**Calming and clarifying**: the balance between tranquility, concentration, sensory discrimination, and insight. See [Calming and Clarifying](calming-and-clarifying.md).

**Practice cycles**: stillness, motion, life practice, micro-hits, challenge sequences, retreat or equivalent, feedback, and long-term follow-through. See [Practice Cycles and Life Architecture](practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md).

**Practice planning loop**: a repeatable way to choose an aim, choose a container, practice, read the report, adjust, and check life effects over a week or month. See [Practice Planning Loop](practice-planning-loop.md).

**Practice report check**: a six-part way to make experience routable: context, sensory facts, CCE quality, reaction, life effect, and boundary. See [Practice Report Check](practice-report-check.md).

**Asking for guidance**: a bounded way to ask a teacher, coach, peer, or helper for a practice hypothesis without handing over scope, privacy, consent, ordinary care, or judgment. See [Asking for Guidance](asking-for-guidance.md).

**State-to-action integration**: the narrow practice edge where simple needed action can sometimes arise from a deep, open, no-self, or samadhi-like state while task contact, ordinary judgment, and responsiveness remain intact. See [Practice Cycles and Life Architecture](practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md) and [Practice Method Safety](practice-method-safety.md).

**Micro-hit**: a short, deliberate practice contact during a low-demand life moment, such as a few seconds of See/Hear/Feel, Rest, Flow, or reaction noticing. See [Practice Cycles and Life Architecture](practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md).

**Challenge sequence**: gradually training a method from stillness into eyes open, standing, movement, simple tasks, and more complex life situations while preserving ordinary safety and responsiveness. See [Practice Cycles and Life Architecture](practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md) and [Practice Method Safety](practice-method-safety.md).

**Retreat and aftercare**: the route for integrating retreat, long-sit, or intense-practice aftereffects through sleep, food, rhythm, feedback, practice reports, behavior checks, and safety boundaries. See [Retreat and Aftercare](retreat-and-aftercare.md).

**Windows and Walls**: Shinzen's language for openings and obstacles that can become practice-routing moments when ordinary situation, behavior, sensory challenge, support, and safety are all named. See [Windows and Walls](windows-and-walls.md).

**Applied life boundaries**: the route map for illness, sleep, sexuality, birth and parenting, caffeine or substances, lucid dreams, public suffering, and retreat aftereffects when ordinary care and scope limits should lead before technique. See [Applied Life Boundaries](applied-life-boundaries.md).

**Auto Output Practice**: Shinzen's family of practices for noticing body movement, speech or chant, thought, or combined output organizing itself while CCE and ordinary safety remain intact. See [Auto Output Practice](auto-output-practice.md).

**Method switching or branching**: choosing one technique, a planned sequence, or a different route when interest, opportunity, or necessity clearly changes, while watching for overchoice, avoidance, and constant optimization. See [Choosing a Practice Route](choosing-a-practice-route.md) and [Practice Cycles and Life Architecture](practice-cycles-and-life-architecture.md).

**Recycle the reaction**: practicing with the reaction to a practice effect, such as craving, fear, disappointment, state-rebound, relational aversion, void flatness, fascination, or retreat aftershock, when that reaction is workable. See [Recycle the Reaction](recycle-the-reaction.md).

**Complete experience**: experience contacted with enough CCE over time that it becomes less binding and more learning-bearing. See [Complete Experience](complete-experience.md).

**Insight and purification**: Shinzen's paired transformation output: clearer seeing plus the gradual unwinding of old craving, aversion, confusion, and suffering when experience is met with CCE over time. See [Insight and Purification](insight-and-purification.md).

**Suffering distortion cycle**: the loop where tangled sensory components amplify suffering and distort behavior, while CCE can make response clearer. See [The Suffering Distortion Cycle](suffering-distortion-cycle.md).

**Condition-independent happiness**: deep fulfillment less dependent on conditions, produced in Shinzen's model by complete sensory experience rather than by ignoring ordinary needs. See [Condition-Independent Happiness](condition-independent-happiness.md).

**Impermanence**: the practiceable detection of sensory change, not just the belief that things pass. In this atlas it routes through ordinary changingness, Flow, Gone, no-self, and complete experience while keeping Source claims tiered. See [Impermanence](impermanence.md), [Rest, Flow, Gone](rest-flow-gone.md), and [Flow and Gone](flow-and-gone.md).

**Flow**: movement, change, vibration, force, expansion, or contraction within sensory experience. See [Flow and Gone](flow-and-gone.md).

**Gone**: the noticed moment when all or part of a sensory event vanishes or drops away. See [Flow and Gone](flow-and-gone.md).

**No-self**: not the destruction of personality, but the recognition that self-experience is constructed from sensory events. See [No-Self Without Erasing Personality](no-self-without-erasing-personality.md).

**Source or Zero**: Shinzen's deepest boundary language around disappearance, arising, and non-objective origin. See [Source, Zero, and Speculation](source-zero-and-speculation.md).

**Source, service, and bodhicitta**: the service-facing side of Source language, where emptiness, care, behavior, and ordinary accountability must mature together. See [Source, Service, and Bodhicitta](source-service-and-bodhicitta.md).

**Bodhicitta**: the aspiration toward service and care held together with emptiness, without savior identity or world-denial. See [Source, Service, and Bodhicitta](source-service-and-bodhicitta.md).

**Source, science, and analogy**: the boundary that keeps physics, biology, mathematics, neuroscience, AI, nature, and time-space analogies in the right claim tier. See [Source, Science, and Analogy](source-science-and-analogy.md).

**Source proof claims**: claims that Source, love, unity, science, metaphysics, or teacher authority has been proven by a practice experience. Start by sorting the claim tier, then check behavior, service, safety, and ordinary evidence. See [Source, Science, and Analogy](source-science-and-analogy.md), [Source and Claim Tiers](source-and-claim-tiers.md), and [Behavior and Service Test](behavior-and-service-test.md).

**Surface to Source path map**: a non-status-ladder orientation from ordinary self/world experience through sensory untangling, Flow, Gone, Source or Zero language, and return to service. See [Surface to Source Path Map](surface-to-source-path-map.md).

**Expansion and Contraction**: force-pattern labels for Flow: spreading, scattering, increasing, squeezing, gripping, decreasing, or both at once. See [Expansion and Contraction](expansion-and-contraction.md).

**Spaciousness**: openness around or thinness within what is seen, heard, or felt. See [Spaciousness](spaciousness.md).

**Dissolution or bhanga**: a breakup phase where ordinary sensory order may loosen into blissful vibration, harsh chaos, or both. If it is happening now, route through practice, safety, support, and ordinary functioning together. See [Dissolution and Bhanga](dissolution-and-bhanga.md).

**Self-inquiry or Turn Back**: a fit-dependent practice that turns awareness back toward the apparent experiencer, without treating verbal answers or a fixed witness as the endpoint. See [Self-Inquiry and Turn Back](self-inquiry-and-turn-back.md).

**Total Happiness**: surface and deep happiness for self and others. See [Total Happiness](total-happiness.md) and [Total Happiness Aim Structure](total-happiness-aim-structure.md).

**Total Happiness aim structure**: Shinzen's fuller aim map: three jobs, four quadrants, five applications, ordinary and extraordinary happiness, and the rule that improving self/world can support transcendence. See [Total Happiness Aim Structure](total-happiness-aim-structure.md).

**Behavior change escalation**: the route for when practice reveals an urge or pattern but the behavior keeps repeating; it combines sensory urge practice with commitments, feedback, outside support, and safety gates. See [Behavior Change Escalation](behavior-change-escalation.md).

**Teaching as service**: the Total Happiness branch where practice may help others through subtle presence, clear description, explicit instruction, professional teaching, advanced competence, path discovery, or support for teachers. See [The Teacher and the Lineage](teacher-and-lineage.md), [Guidance, Scope, and Accountability](guidance-scope-and-accountability.md), and [Behavior and Service Test](behavior-and-service-test.md).

**Deconstruction and reconstruction**: the balance between dissolving self-fixation and cultivating wholesome Feel, Image, Talk, behavior, and service. See [Deconstruction and Reconstruction](deconstruction-and-reconstruction.md).

**Guidance, scope, and accountability**: the boundary around teacher roles, consent, referral, feedback, behavior verification, dependency, and ordinary support. See [Guidance, Scope, and Accountability](guidance-scope-and-accountability.md).

**Safety and scope**: the route question for when the atlas can help with practice framing and when ordinary care, protection, referral, accountability, or stopping should govern first. See [Safety, Scope, and Accountability](safety-scope-and-accountability.md).

**Practice method safety**: the question of whether a technique is increasing workable CCE or becoming strain, spaciness, overchoice, avoidance, compulsive monitoring, or unsupported exposure. See [Practice Method Safety](practice-method-safety.md).

**Intensity and embodiment safety**: the boundary around pain, emotion, Strong Determination, illness, ritual heat, kriyas, retreat aftershock, and ordinary body care. See [Intensity and Embodiment Safety](intensity-and-embodiment-safety.md).

**DPDR and the pit of the void**: the safety distinction between liberating no-self or emptiness and depersonalization-derealization-like distress, nihilism, or loss of function. See [DPDR and the Pit of the Void](dpdr-and-the-pit-of-the-void.md).

**Altered phenomena and dissolution safety**: the boundary for visions, powers, Flow energy, Gone, bhanga, Source afterglow, lucid dreams, and unusual states. See [Altered Phenomena and Dissolution Safety](altered-phenomena-and-dissolution-safety.md).

**Operational enlightenment**: a practice-facing way to discuss liberation as reduced identity-capture by thought and body sensation, without implying perfection or teacher authority. See [Operational Enlightenment](operational-enlightenment.md).

**Mastery without guru inflation**: the rule that contemplative depth can be real and component-specific without proving conduct, safety, map finality, or total authority. See [The Teacher and the Lineage](teacher-and-lineage.md).

## How To Use This Glossary

Use the glossary only as an entry point. Shinzen's terms usually do work inside a method or route. "Gone" is not just an interesting word; it changes what the practitioner notices. "Equanimity" is not just calm; it changes the relationship to arising and passing. "Source" is not a license to make metaphysical or ethical claims without support.
